Since 2018

A small studio with a big love for detail

Nuso Jewels began in a small corner of a family home in Abraka, born from a simple frustration — beautiful, well-made jewellery always seemed out of reach. So we decided to make it ourselves.

Today, every piece that leaves our studio is still finished by hand. We work with responsibly sourced metals, freshwater pearls, and natural stones, and each design is tested for comfort, wearability, and the kind of quiet elegance that doesn't shout for attention.

We believe jewellery should be worn, not stored. It should travel with you through ordinary days and extraordinary ones — and still feel like yours years from now.
